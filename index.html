Kimbo's Birthday Trail Lab for the COROS Apex 4

Exploring the trails of Oregon, from the Pacific Coast & Cascades to the High Desert

I've set up a few tasks to help you get familiar with using maps and your new watch to plan trail runs in places you've never been before. You'll learn how to draw a route, export it as a GPX file, download a GPX file, and load it onto your watch. For the final challenge, you'll build a run somewhere beautiful beyond the city, including a big point-to-point on Mount Hood or a sunny winter run on the dry east side of the Gorge.

Mission 2 · Build in the COROS app

Draw a 3 mile run through the forest

This practice phone behaves like the real flow: draw on the map, save the route, find it in your Route Library, then sync it to the watch. The map is a simplified sketch of Forest Park, not for navigation.

Done

Goal: a route of at least 3 miles that you send to your watch. In the Coros app navigate to the Explore Tab and Create a route, and send to your watch.

Try turning on "Snap" so your points jump onto the nearest trail. Real route builders do the same, which keeps you off cliffs and out of blackberries - most of the time.
